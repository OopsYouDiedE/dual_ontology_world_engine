# 参考重绘提示词

生成方式：内置 imagegen。第一输入为已查看的网上动作帧参考，第二输入为本项目五方设计。链接与抽帧方式见《动作参考.md》。

## 长旌旗

Use case: style-transfer. Generate a REDRAWN production sprite animation atlas. INPUT 1 is the actual four-frame Quiet hanging-banner animation from TWD_BALDI Modular_Banner_16x16: study ONLY its movement, static top attachment, shifting side fold and lightly flexing hem. INPUT 2 is our OWN five-faction art; use ONLY its top row identities, colors and scale, not its old animation.
OUTPUT: exactly EIGHT columns of successive animation phases, FIVE rows of factions, 40 sprites. Every logical cell is 48x96, final atlas logical384x480, render crisp nearest neighbor 3x preferred1152x1440. ACTUAL transparent alpha background, no labels, no lines, no walls or floor. Every sprite fully contained and uniformly aligned in its cell. Uniform identical drawing scale.
Each narrow hanging pennon: fixed horizontal rod across x6..41 at y8; fixed fabric top x11..36 y12; soft cloth falls to y85 in a simple point. The embroidery is SMALL, occupies just central 14x19px at about y44. No thick gold edging; broad areas of unadorned cloth above and below the motif. Native hard-edged game pixels, limited palette, 3 shade bands per cloth, no photorealistic microtexture.
Rows top-to-bottom: gold Chinese round coiled dragon on vermilion; gold flaming torch/open laurel on crimson; silver triangle eye/cloud-scroll base on navy; silver volcano/ocean waves on charcoal; gold heraldic lion on burgundy. Use the SAME simplified icon in all frames within its row, NO icon redesign, no random shape changes or emblem crawling.
ANIMATION: emulate the quiet reference's stationary top and evolving side folds, interpolate into 8 coherent phases across the row. Slow 1-2 native-pixel swaying of the lower third, diminishing continuously to ZERO displacement at top. A broad soft vertical fold slowly changes width across the cloth, subtly compressing the embroidered motif with it. Hem trails the movement of the middle cloth. Phase progression: centered moving right, slightly right, rightmost, returning, centered moving left, slightly left, leftmost, returning. Not rigid-body sliding or rotating. Keep top edge and rod exact same pixels. Four discrete phase directions need actual coherent in-between frames. Frame7 returns smoothly into0, without duplicate terminal frame. Clear small differences at the silhouette; shading moves in concert, not random flashing. Orthographic straight-on view. No new decorative details, no extra rows or columns.

## 横旗

Use case: style-transfer. Redraw a production 48px RPG FLAG ANIMATION using ACTUAL reference motion.
INPUT 1: a motion-reference contact strip sampled at phases0,2,4,6,8,10,12,14 from Screaming Brain Studios' 16-frame orthographic Animated Flags Pack. It shows ONE traveling wave: observe how top and bottom cloth contours move together and how the three vertical colored regions COMPRESS and EXPAND with the same fold. Use this phase progression. Do NOT copy French flag colors or rectangular background.
INPUT 2: our own faction artwork, use its middle row emblems and fabric colors only. Its old motion is to be replaced.
OUTPUT EXACTLY 8 columns by5 rows, 40 isolated flags on actual transparent alpha background. Each cell48x48 logical pixels; total384x240 logical, rendered4x nearest neighbor ideally1536x960. Eight phases in reference order left to right. Five faction rows: gold circular Chinese dragon on vermilion, gold torch/laurel on crimson, silver triangle eye/cloud on navy, silver volcano/waves on charcoal, gold heraldic lion on burgundy. SAME emblem identity in every frame; no random redraw/morph or shape replacement.
Strict orthographic side elevation, rectangle-shaped cloth with shallow fork at right. Slim vertical flagpole stays EXACTLY fixed at x6,y5..43 within EVERY cell. Attachments fixed x8,y12 and y32. Cloth extends to x40 with margin; three gentle discrete shade bands, not gradients. Central small motif 13x14px in the middle portion of cloth, plenty of blank cloth. No large gold borders.
Motion must READ AS FLYING CLOTH: wave travels from the fixed left hoist to the free right fly; phased top and bottom undulate together, no arbitrary widening triangle. Fold develops on left, crosses the emblem zone, reaches tip, exits, then next fold. Free tip rises and falls by3-4 native pixels, small apparent width contraction/expansion 2-3 pixels as fold turns in depth. Emblem undergoes the SAME local compression and shading as underlying cloth, never rotates or crawls on it. Fix the left edge so only the free area moves. Not rigid rotation/translation or 8 random silhouettes. Frame7 connects to frame0. High correspondence of pixels and recognizable emblem between adjacent phases; no moving camera, perspective or pole.
Crisp deliberately low-resolution pixel clusters, 3-4 shades per fabric, no painterly microtextures, no glowing metal, no antialias or blur. Uniform equal grid with identical anchors and scale, no labels/text or background, no cut-off sprites.
