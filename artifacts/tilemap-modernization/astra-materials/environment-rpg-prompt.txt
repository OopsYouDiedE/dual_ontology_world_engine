Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a refined original 2D RPG material-and-furniture sample board, landscape, on warm off-white. This is a new independent art direction, not a copy of previous images.

CRITICAL PROJECTION CONTRACT:
Classic RPG angled top-down PARALLEL projection, like a flat front-aligned tilemap. NOT a 90-degree overhead plan; NOT isometric; NOT a three-quarter view; NO perspective camera. Furniture and architecture show exactly TWO surfaces: the TOP and the FRONT facing the bottom of the image. NEVER show a LEFT or RIGHT side surface. No converging lines or vanishing point.
Every tabletop is a perfectly axis-aligned rectangle: equal-length horizontal back and front edges, equal-length vertical left and right edges, four right angles. The top is a visible rectangle, with a FRONT fascia descending straight vertically from its bottom edge. Front legs descend vertically below this front fascia. Its left and right edges have NO side face extending diagonally. No trapezoids, no diamonds, no side wedges. Vertical height is projected straight down on the page; all horizontal furniture axes remain screen horizontal/vertical. Apply exactly this same projection to chairs, walls, doorway, and floor.

Layout: Two spacious areas, no characters and no scene narrative.
LEFT, about 70%: a small finished dining-room material vignette occupying a large clear rectangle. Back wall across the top: a narrow visible wall TOP strip above a broad flat FRONT elevation, absolutely no lateral side faces, no corner walls receding toward the viewer. Pale warm plaster over a low walnut wainscot, quiet broad fields. Centered straight doorway opening in the back wall, no perspective inside the doorway. Ground below is a rectangular level field of medium warm brown long floorboards; horizontal grain, staggered sparse joints, broad wood structure, not a dense noisy repeated checkerboard.
On this floor: ONE beautiful rectangular walnut dining table centered with a warm lighter brown tabletop, a restrained narrow darker edge, a visible slim FRONT apron and TWO vertical front legs; top rectangle approximately twice as wide as deep. No food or tabletop objects, so its projection is unmistakable.
TWO matching wooden chairs appear as separate assets placed on the lower left and lower right of the table with ample clearance, both front-aligned toward the bottom of the screen. Each chair shows its rectangular seat TOP, flat front seat rail, two straight front legs and flat backrest, no side planes, no diagonal sides, no rotation. Chair construction simple, mature, slightly softened corners, sensible clear silhouettes.
RIGHT, about 30%: three neatly separated swatches vertically: broad long walnut floorboard material, warm-gray large stone slab material, short pale plaster-and-walnut wall module showing TOP STRIP plus FRONT FACE. Short exact labels only: "木地板", "石地面", "墙体". No other text.

Style: thoughtful premium hand-painted pixel-art game assets. Crisp purposeful pixel clusters and clean silhouettes, restrained warm colors, about 24 harmonious colors, painterly material economy rather than dense photographic noise. Floor deliberately lower contrast than furniture; table and chair outlines easy to read at small game scale. Calm plaster, warm medium-brown floor, darker walnut frames and honey-brown furniture tops, subdued stone. Short soft contact shadows directly beneath objects, no dramatic cast shadows.
Avoid clutter, rugs, plants, chandeliers, decorative symbols, gilded ornate curlicues, fantasy props, explanatory arrows, people, photo lighting, thick sticker outlines, tilted presentation cards.
Before finishing audit the table: a visible RECTANGULAR top AND flat FRONT face, zero left/right face visible. This must not accidentally become a pure overhead plan again.
